_A from-scratch Modula-2 that treats the modern Windows platform — Win32, COM,
Direct3D, WinMM audio — as first-class, reachable from pure Modula-2, with COM
vtables the compiler proves correct by construction._

## TL;DR

- **What:** the **canonical Windows Modula-2** — a Windows-native compiler + runtime
  (PIM 4 + ISO 10514-1) on **Rust + LLVM 22.1**, ORC JIT (`newm2 run`) and AOT
  `.exe` (`newm2 build`).
- **Reach:** pure Modula-2 drives **Win32, a machine-checked COM object model,
  Direct3D 11 + HLSL, Direct2D/DirectWrite, GDI, and WinMM audio/MIDI** — up to a
  full game engine (D3D + synth + MIDI at 60 fps) in one program.
- **Self-hosted:** two IDEs **written in Modula-2** — FastM2 and the GPU-pane
  FastPanesM2.
- **Where it goes:** the direct Windows predecessor of the Cocoa-native
  [MacModula2](/posts/macmodula2).
- **Get it:** [Downloads](#download--run) · [Source](https://github.com/albanread/WindowsModula2)

## What it is

> _From the README/MANIFESTO —_ "the Modula-2 member of a portfolio of from-scratch

> and ISO 10514-1 specifications, "inspired by the ADW Modula-2 distribution,"
> targeting "Modern Windows, deliberately and exclusively" (macOS/Linux out of
> scope for v1).

## How it works

- **Pipeline:** Modula-2 → LLVM IR → **ORC JIT** (RTDyld + Win64 SEH registration)
  and AOT PE/COFF `.exe`, with an `--opt 0..3` LLVM pipeline.
- **Memory:** **classical manual memory** (`HeapAlloc`/`HeapFree`, every
  `NEW`/`DISPOSE` paired) — the earlier GC-by-default design was dropped in the
  restart (`docs/m2new-port-assessment.md`, the "manual-memory pivot").
- **Machine-checked COM:** an `INTERFACE` declaration carries an IID and a
  per-method `@ordinal` pragma; the compiler computes each vtable slot by walking
  the `INHERIT` chain and rejects mismatches at compile time (`GUARD`/`ISMEMBER` via
  `QueryInterface`) — "COM that (probably) cannot be wrong by construction."
- **Platform stack in pure M2:** Direct3D 11 + HLSL (`ShaderView`),
  Direct2D/DirectWrite (`Canvas2D`, `TermRender`), GDI (`RasterView`, `Chart`), a
  retro game mode (`GameViewGpu`: palette framebuffer, sprites, parallax), Win32
  windowing (`WinShell`), WinMM audio/music (`Audio`, `WaveOut`, `Abc`/`MidiOut`/
  `SmfFile`), and a reactive `PaneShell` GUI framework. Win32/COM bindings are
  generated by `newm2-winapi-gen` from a SQLite projection of `.winmd`.
- **Language surface:** ISO EXCEPTIONS on Win64 SEH, ISO/PIM COROUTINES on Win32
  fibres, first-class SIMD lane types (`REAL32X4`/`REAL64X2`), narrow/wide
  (ANSI/UTF-16) strings.
