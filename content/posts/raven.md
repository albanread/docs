_Fair warning, and the label is the point: **Raven is not a compiler, and
nothing on this page is engineering I am shipping.** It is design fiction —
a concept document — kept on this blog because the repo set is accounted
for here in full, and because an engineering culture that never speculates
is only doing half its job._

## What it actually is

From its own README: "A hybrid cybernetic system for autonomous drones and
androids. Raven fuses cultured corvid neural tissue with conventional
silicon compute into a single distributed intelligence…"

A worldbuilding exercise, in other words — the kind of document one writes
to find out what one thinks, wearing the costume of a technical
specification. Corvids because they are the right birds for it: small
brains, unreasonable competence, a well-documented grudge capacity.

## Why it's on a compiler blog

Two reasons, one dull and one less so. The dull one: this site accounts for
the whole repository set, and silently omitting the odd one out would be a
small dishonesty of the kind that grows. The less dull one: the same
imagination that asks "what if a Forth class *were* an Objective-C object?"
occasionally asks sillier questions, and I see no reason to pretend
otherwise. The discipline is not *never speculate*; it is *label the
speculation* — which this page does in its title, its status line, and now
twice in its body.

There are no binaries. There is no download table. The crows remain, for
the moment, unencumbered by firmware.
